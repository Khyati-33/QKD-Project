# Manuscript package

`main.tex` is the IEEEtran manuscript. Its only empirical architecture comparison is matched GNN-PPO versus LSTM-PPO. The four primary held-out plots are generated from `experiments/final_gnn_lstm_holdout_20261007.json`; the selected-seed reward-component audit is a separate fifth diagnostic figure. The matched architecture diagram, topology map, and physical-channel diagram provide the method context. Figures use standard IEEE single- and double-column floats near their first substantive references.

The publication bundle contains the matched protocol, final comparison outputs, figure-generation sources, manuscript figures, and the supplementary figure archive. Earlier GNN-versus-route-baseline and GNN-versus-MLP evidence is retained in the local research archive only and excluded from both the manuscript and the Overleaf bundle.

The results are simulator-only. Corridor geometry is an OpenStreetMap proxy; channel and key-pool settings are not calibrated field measurements. Normalized rate is not delivered secret-key throughput. Replace the draft author block and complete funding and acknowledgment details before submission.
